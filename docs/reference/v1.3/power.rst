Power Distribution
##################

The LimeSDR XTRX board may be powered via the USB port (5 V) or the Mini PCIe edge connector (3.3 V). The power delivery network consists of different power rails/voltages, filters and power sequences. The LimeSDR XTRX board power distribution block diagram is presented in Figure 9.
